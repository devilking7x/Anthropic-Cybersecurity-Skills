# Standards and References — Incident Response

Real standards, frameworks, and guidance for the procedures in `SKILL.md`. Nothing here is a placeholder.

## Frameworks and lifecycle models

- NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 — Computer Security Incident Handling Guide: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final
- SANS PICERL model — Preparation, Identification, Containment, Eradication, Recovery, Lessons Learned: https://www.sans.org/white-papers/39100/
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
- CISA — Incident Response guidance and the StopRansomware Guide: https://www.cisa.gov/topics/cyber-threats-and-advisories

## Notification obligations

- GDPR Article 33 — Notification of a personal data breach to the supervisory authority (72 hours): https://gdpr-info.eu/art-33-gdpr/
- GDPR Article 34 — Communication of a personal data breach to the data subject: https://gdpr-info.eu/art-34-gdpr/
- US state breach-notification laws (typically 30–60 days) and sector rules — check the jurisdiction and contracts that apply before an incident, not during one.
