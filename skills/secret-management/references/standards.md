# Standards and References — Secret Management

Real standards, frameworks, and tool documentation for the procedures in `SKILL.md`. Nothing here is a placeholder.

## Standards and guidance

- OWASP — Secrets Management Cheat Sheet: https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Secrets_Management_Cheat_Sheet.html
- NIST SP 800-57 Part 1 Rev. 5 — Recommendation for Key Management: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/57/pt1/r5/final
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — Security and Privacy Controls (IA, SC families): https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final
